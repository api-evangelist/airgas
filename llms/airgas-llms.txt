## Agent-relevant summary

Airgas publishes **no public API**. There is no OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL, gRPC, AsyncAPI or WSDL
contract on any Airgas host, no developer portal, no SDK in any package registry, no MCP server and no
A2A agent card. `api.airgas.com`, `developer.airgas.com`, `developers.airgas.com`, `apis.airgas.com`,
`punchout.airgas.com`, `ebusiness.airgas.com` and `status.airgas.com` do not resolve in DNS. Every
`/.well-known/` path on `www.airgas.com` returns HTTP 404.

Machine-to-machine integration with Airgas is **B2B procurement integration, arranged commercially**:
cXML or OCI punchout, hosted catalogs, inbound order integration, and outbound order confirmation, ASN
and invoice documents over cXML or EDI. Airgas states this on its eBusiness Solutions page and routes
the technical setup through its eBusiness Solutions team via a contact form. An agent cannot self-serve
an Airgas integration; a human trading-partner onboarding is required.
